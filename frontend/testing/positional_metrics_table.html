MVPA Positional Performance Metrics Matrix

Position Statistic Category Reason for Inclusion
Attacking Expected Goals (xG) per 90 Goal Threat Measures the quality and volume of shots taken (primary indicator).
Attacking Non-Penalty Goals (NPG) per 90 Goal Output Measures open-play scoring efficiency.
Attacking Expected Assists (xA) per 90 Creation Measures the quality of chances created for teammates.
Attacking Goal-Creating Actions (GCA) Creation Measures offensive actions directly leading to a goal.
Attacking Progressive Carries (PCarries) per 90 Progression Measures direct, forward movement of the ball.
Attacking Successful Dribbles (%) per 90 Efficiency Measures flair and close control effectiveness.
Attacking Shots on Target Rate (%) per 90 Efficiency Measures shot accuracy relative to attempts.
Attacking Touches in the Opponent's Box per 90 Positioning Measures presence in high-danger scoring zones.
Midfield Expected Assists (xA) per 90 Creation Measures passing quality leading to shot opportunities.
Midfield Key Passes per 90 Creation Passes that directly lead to a shot by a teammate.
Midfield Pass Completion Rate (%) Retention Measures ball security in the midfield third.
Midfield Progressive Passes (PPasses) per 90 Progression Measures volume of line-breaking passes.
Midfield Tackles Won (%) Defense Measures efficiency in regaining possession.
Midfield Successful Pressures (%) Defense Measures pressure effectiveness within five seconds.
Midfield Interceptions per 90 Defense Measures reading the game and breaking up attacks.
Midfield Miscontrols and Dispossessed per 90 Ball Security Measures loss of possession under pressure (negative factor).
Defensive Aerial Duel Success Rate (%) Physicality Measures dominance in air battles (crucial for CBs).
Defensive Defensive Actions (DA) in Final Third per 90 Positioning Measures proactive defending higher up the pitch.
Defensive Ball Recoveries per 90 Possession Measures how quickly a player wins back loose balls.
Defensive Progressive Passing Distance (PPD) per 90 Progression Measures ground gained through passing (ball-playing CBs).
Defensive Long Pass Completion Rate (%) Build-up Measures accuracy in launching attacks from the back.
Defensive Blocks (Shots/Passes) per 90 Reliability Measures body positioning and blocking ability.
Defensive Tackles/Interceptions Attempted per 90 Activity Measures the volume of defensive engagement.
Defensive Clearances per 90 Clearance Measures effective removal of danger from the box.
Goalkeeper PSxG-GA per 90 Shot-Stopping Compares expected goals faced (after the shot) to goals conceded.
Goalkeeper Save Percentage Shot-Stopping Traditional baseline of goalkeeper effectiveness.
Goalkeeper Crosses Stopped (%) Command Ability to proactively claim or punch crosses.
Goalkeeper Launch Completion Rate (%) Distribution Accuracy of goal kicks and long throws.
Goalkeeper Passes Completed Short/Medium (%) Distribution Accuracy in initiating build-up play from the back.
Goalkeeper DAOTB per 90 Sweeper Proactive positioning to clear danger outside the box.
